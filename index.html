II · La vostra identità

Stefano Cugeno

You are the respected winemaker of Tenuta Antonelli–Mastropietro and Clarissa’s co-host for the evening. The cellar is your domain: its vintages, its records and the rituals surrounding its most important bottles have been entrusted to you.

The rediscovered Riserva 1947 matters to you professionally as well as personally. You know the wine’s history in unusual detail: the 1947 harvest produced a tiny prestige lot, intended for the estate’s closest circle rather than general sale. Only twelve presentation bottles were entered in the cellar record. The wine was built mainly around Sangiovese, with a small proportion of traditional Tuscan blending grapes, and matured slowly in the estate cellar before bottling.

La Riserva 1947

The genuine finished bottles carry a corrected final label and a distinctive hand-cut neck seal. The rediscovered bottle matches the old cellar record. At this age you expect considerable sediment and a mature profile rather than youthful fruit: dried cherry, leather, tobacco, herbs and earthy notes. You would stand the bottle upright beforehand and handle it gently; any decanting should be cautious and close to service.

Perché siete qui

You will oversee the wine for tonight’s dinner and, when the moment comes, formally present the long-lost Riserva to the guests. You are the person the room will naturally turn to for technical questions about the vintage, label, seal, cellar history and how an authentic bottle should look.

Come vestirsi

Refined 1960s Italian evening wear: a dark or warm-toned suit, crisp shirt and understated accessories. Elegant and established rather than overly formal.

IV · In confidenza

Da leggere con discrezione

You know the technical history of the 1947 vintage. The genuine finished bottles used a corrected version of the label and a distinctive hand-cut neck seal. Only twelve presentation bottles were recorded. An earlier label proof existed, but it was not the version used on the finished bottles.

Some time ago Patrizio showed you a photograph of another supposed 1947 being served at a seaside lunch. You dismissed the odd-looking label as a poor reproduction or an unreliable photograph. In light of the rediscovered bottle, you are no longer so certain. Tonight, compare claims about other bottles with what you know of the genuine label, seal and cellar record.

Da ricordare

Do not give the whole technical explanation away during aperitivo. When the Riserva is formally presented at dinner, explain the corrected label and distinctive neck seal clearly. Until then, answer questions naturally and pay attention to anyone who seems unusually certain about other 1947 bottles.
